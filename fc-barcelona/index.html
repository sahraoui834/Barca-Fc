History & philosophy

Swiss-born Joan Gamper founded the club on 29 November 1899. Since then Barça has stood for Catalan identity, solidarity and a way of playing that people recognise within seconds.

Tiki-taka

Short passes, constant movement and control of the ball. Shaped by Johan Cruyff and perfected under Pep Guardiola, it treats possession as the best defence.

La Masia

The academy that raised Xavi, Iniesta, Messi, Busquets and Pedri teaches technique and values together, and keeps feeding the first team.

Més que un club

Barça belongs to its socis, who elect the president. The club has long carried Catalan culture and social causes, from its shirt to its charity work.

Values

Humility, effort, ambition, teamwork and respect. They are taught from the youngest teams up.
